Inbox Overload

Hundreds of tenant emails a week — maintenance requests, lease queries, complaints — all landing in one inbox with no system to triage them.

Missed Maintenance

Requests fall through the cracks. Tenants chase up. Contractors don't get briefed. Small issues become expensive emergencies.

Manual rent reminders, late fees, and follow-ups consume hours every month — time that could be spent growing your portfolio.

Keeping track of inspections, certifications, and regulatory deadlines across multiple properties is a constant source of stress and risk.

Features

Everything automated. Nothing missed.

Noumena covers every repetitive task in your day — so you can focus on the decisions that actually require you.

Email Triage & Auto-Drafting

AI reads, categorises, and drafts responses to incoming emails. You review and approve — or let it run fully automated for routine enquiries.

Tenant Communication

Consistent, professional responses to tenant queries 24/7. Escalations are flagged immediately so nothing urgent slips through.

Rent Collection & Reminders

Automated payment reminders, overdue notices, and escalation sequences. Track rent status across your entire portfolio in one view.

Maintenance Request Routing

Tenants submit requests, our system triages urgency, assigns the right contractor, and tracks resolution — end to end.

Compliance Monitoring

Never miss a gas certificate, EPC renewal, or inspection deadline. Automated alerts and scheduling keep you permanently compliant.

Client Dashboard

A clean, real-time view of your entire portfolio — occupancy, open issues, payments, and upcoming tasks — in one place.
