Features

Everything automated.
Nothing missed.

Noumena covers every repetitive task in your day — so you can focus on the decisions that actually require you.

Email Triage & Auto-Drafting

AI reads, categorises, and drafts responses to incoming emails. You review and approve — or let it run fully automated for routine enquiries.

Tenant Communication Hub

Consistent, professional responses to tenant queries 24/7. Escalations are flagged immediately so nothing urgent slips through.

Rent Collection & Reminders

Automated payment reminders, overdue notices, and escalation sequences. Track rent status across your entire portfolio in one clean view.

Maintenance Request Routing

Tenants submit requests, our system triages urgency, assigns the right contractor, and tracks resolution — end to end without manual intervention.

Compliance Monitoring

Never miss a gas certificate, EPC renewal, or inspection deadline. Automated alerts and scheduling keep you permanently compliant and audit-ready.

Portfolio Dashboard

A clean, real-time view of your entire portfolio — occupancy, open issues, payments, and upcoming tasks — in one place, accessible anywhere.
